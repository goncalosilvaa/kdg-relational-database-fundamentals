- students
    - student_id CHAR(10) PK
    - name       VARCHAR(50) NN
    - street     VARCHAR(100) NN
    - nr         NUMERIC(4) NN
    - postalcode NUMERIC(4) NN
    - city       VARCHAR(50) NN
    - birth_date DATE NN
- student_classes
    - student_id CHAR(10) PK FK1
    - class_id NUMERIC(4) PK FK2
- classes
    - class_id NUMERIC(4) PK
    - building CHAR(2) NN
    - floor    NUMERIC(1) NN
    - roomnumber NUMERIC(2) NN

Business Rules:
- students(nr) must be greater that 0
- students(postalcode) must be between 1000 and 9999
- students(birth_date) must be before today
- classes(building) must be one of the following values: "GR", "PH" or "SW"
- classes(floor) must be positive and may not be greater than 5
- classes(roomnumber) must be positive
